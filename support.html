常见问题

为什么显示"找不到'写点什么'发帖入口"？

可能你还没加入这个群、群组只允许管理员发帖，或者 Facebook 改版了。先在浏览器里打开那个群，确认自己能手动发帖。

为什么任务自动暂停了？

达到每日上限、Facebook 显示了限制或警告、没有登录 Facebook，或者发帖窗口被关闭时，扩展会自动暂停。暂停原因显示在"运行"区域。遇到 Facebook 警告时，建议隔几个小时再继续，并减少发帖数量。

定时排程没有运行？

到时间时 Chrome 必须开着，电脑不能关机或睡眠。晚了 2 小时以上的那一次会被跳过。

测试模式是什么？

测试模式只会把内容填进发帖窗口，不会点"发布"。建议先用测试模式试 1–2 个群，确认没问题再关掉。

我的数据存在哪里？

全部只保存在你自己的浏览器里，详见隐私政策。

Common questions

Why does it say it couldn't find the "Write something…" box?

You may not be a member of that group, the group may only let admins post, or Facebook may have changed its layout. Open the group yourself and check that you can post manually.

Why did the task pause by itself?

It pauses when the daily limit is reached, Facebook shows a restriction or warning, you're logged out of Facebook, or the posting window is closed. The reason is shown under "Run". After a Facebook warning, wait a few hours before resuming and post less.

Why didn't my schedule run?

Chrome must be open and the computer awake at the scheduled time. A run more than 2 hours late is skipped.

What is test mode?

Test mode fills in the post window but doesn't click "Post". Try 1–2 groups in test mode first, then turn it off.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser. See the Privacy Policy.

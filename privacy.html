隐私政策

群发助手（Chrome 扩展） · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

中文English

群发助手是一个 Chrome 扩展，帮助用户把自己写好的帖子，按自己设定的时间和间隔，发到自己已经加入的 Facebook 群组。本政策说明扩展处理哪些数据，以及如何处理。

一句话总结

你的数据只保存在你自己的电脑上。开发者收不到你的群组、帖子、图片、视频、日志或账号信息，也不做任何统计或追踪。

扩展保存的数据

以下数据保存在你电脑上 Chrome 的扩展本地存储（chrome.storage.local）里，只供扩展自己使用：

  • 你添加的群组链接和群组名称，以及每个群上次发送的时间和结果；
  • 你写的帖子文字和添加的图片、视频；
  • 定时排程（时间规则和对应的帖子内容）；
  • 设置（等待时间、每日上限、测试模式、界面语言）；
  • 运行日志（最近 300 条）。

这些数据不会上传到开发者或任何第三方的服务器。

扩展在 Facebook 页面上做什么

  • 只在你开始操作时运行：只有在你开始发帖任务、某个排程到时间，或者点"识别已加入的群组"时，扩展才会打开 facebook.com 的页面。
  • 做什么：在页面里填入你写好的内容并点"发布"，或者读取"你的群组"页面上的群组链接和名称。
  • 在哪里处理：全部在你自己的浏览器里完成。扩展用的是你在 Chrome 里已经登录的 Facebook 账号，不会读取、保存或传送你的密码。
  • 不会碰的内容：扩展不会读取你的私信、好友列表或其他个人资料。

网络请求

  • 广告配置：扩展可能会定期从开发者的网址下载一个广告配置文件（纯数据，不是程序代码），用来在控制面板里显示广告。这个请求不附带任何关于你的信息。和访问任何网站一样，提供文件的服务器会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • 广告和外部链接：控制面板里的广告和"请作者喝杯咖啡"等链接，只有在你点击时才会打开第三方网站。那些网站有各自的隐私政策。部分广告是推广链接（会标明"推广链接"），通过它购买时，开发者可能获得佣金。
  • 除此之外，扩展不会把数据发送到任何地方。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集个人身份信息、联系方式、财务信息、健康信息、位置信息或浏览记录；
  • 不使用统计分析工具，不追踪你的使用行为；
  • 不出售、出租或转让任何用户数据；
  • 不把数据用于判断信用或发放贷款。

删除数据

你可以在控制面板里删除群组、清除结果、清空日志、删除排程。卸载扩展时，Chrome 会删除扩展保存的全部数据。

儿童

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的数据。

与 Facebook 和 Meta 的关系

本扩展是独立开发的工具，与 Meta Platforms, Inc. 或 Facebook 没有任何关联，也没有获得它们的认可或赞助。你需要自己遵守 Facebook 的服务条款和各个群组的规则。过量或重复发帖，可能导致你的账号被 Facebook 限制。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，会更新本页面和上面的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Group Poster (Chrome extension) · Effective date: November 26, 2026

Group Poster is a Chrome extension that helps you publish posts you wrote yourself to Facebook groups you have joined, at the times and intervals you choose. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

In short

Your data stays on your own computer. The developer never receives your groups, posts, images, videos, logs or account details, and the extension does no analytics or tracking.

Data the extension stores

The following is kept in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer and used only by the extension:

  • the group links and names you add, and when each group was last posted to and with what result;
  • the post text, images and videos you add;
  • your schedules (time rules and their post content);
  • your settings (wait times, daily limit, test mode, interface language);
  • a log of the last 300 events.

None of this is uploaded to the developer or to any third party.

What the extension does on Facebook pages

  • Only when you act: the extension opens facebook.com pages only when you start a posting task, when one of your schedules is due, or when you click "Find my groups".
  • What it does there: it fills in your post and clicks "Post", or reads the group links and names on your "Your groups" page.
  • Where it happens: everything runs inside your own browser, using the Facebook account you are already logged in to in Chrome. The extension never reads, stores or transmits your password.
  • What it doesn't touch: it does not read your messages, friends list or other profile information.

Network requests

  • Ad configuration: the extension may periodically download an ad configuration file from the developer's website. The file is plain data, not program code, and is used to show ads in the dashboard. The request contains no information about you. As with any website, the server that provides the file can see your IP address.
  • Ads and outside links: ads and links such as "Buy the author a coffee" in the dashboard open third-party websites only when you click them. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some ads are affiliate links and are labelled as such; the developer may earn a commission if you buy through them.
  • Apart from this, the extension sends no data anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personally identifiable, contact, financial, health or location information, or browsing history.
  • We don't use analytics or track how you use the extension.
  • We don't sell, rent or transfer any user data.
  • We don't use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Deleting your data

You can delete groups, clear results, clear the log and delete schedules in the dashboard. Uninstalling the extension makes Chrome delete all of its stored data.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Relationship with Facebook and Meta

This extension is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. or Facebook. You are responsible for following Facebook's Terms of Service and each group's rules. Posting too much or too repetitively may lead Facebook to restrict your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.
